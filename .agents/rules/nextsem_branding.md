# Nextsem Academy Branding Guidelines

When generating, capturing, processing, or embedding screenshots and tutorial images:

1. **Strictly Monochromatic**:
   - The watermark (logo icon, border, and text) must be strictly monochrome (white, silver, and subtle grayscale).
   - Do NOT use color (e.g., no green or colored tints).

2. **Empty Negative Space Placement**:
   - Place the watermark strictly in large empty/negative spaces within the screenshot (such as the open black terminal void in the lower-right area well below prompts and text).
   - Never place the watermark over or horizontally adjacent to active commands, flags, prompts, or output text.

3. **Styling & Proportions**:
   - Compact semi-transparent dark pill background (`rgba(12, 15, 20, 0.65)`).
   - Subtle silver border (`rgba(210, 215, 225, 0.45)`).
   - Monochromatic "NS" rounded square logo.
   - Clean anti-aliased font for "Nextsem Academy".
